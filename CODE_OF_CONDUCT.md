# 🕊️ Code of Conduct

Evolve is a language about learning from failure. Our community should be the same: **a safe place to be wrong, ask, and grow.**

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## 🌿 Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## ✅ Expected behavior

- 🤝 Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with mistakes.
- 🍂 Treat failures, including your own, as data, not as a reason to blame.
- 🔬 Critique ideas, never people. Bring evidence where you can.
- 🙏 Accept constructive feedback gracefully, and give it generously.
- 🌍 Remember that contributors come from many languages and cultures.

## ❌ Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments of any kind.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or sustained disruption.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## 🛡️ Enforcement

Community maintainers will remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they consider harmful.

**Reporting:** contact the maintainers privately through the [MAISON organization](https://github.com/maison-protocol) or a private GitHub security advisory on the repository. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Consequences follow the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): correction, warning, temporary ban, and permanent ban.

## 📜 Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and chat) and when an individual officially represents the community in public spaces.
