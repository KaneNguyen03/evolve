## New ideas from this session

- 🌱 **`peace explain <lesson-id>`**: show a Lesson with the code that failed, similar past Lessons, and the fixes that worked.
- 🌱 **Lesson-driven test generation**: every resolved Lesson automatically becomes a regression example in the gene's fitness (adopted in RFC 0003 as "regression Lessons").
- 🌱 **Python-spelling hints**: the parser accepts `True`, `**`, and `lambda` only to suggest the Peace spelling (adopted in RFC 0001).
